About Laura

The Dinner Party That Started It All.

Savor & Simplify is a curated guide to the products that make every season feel a little more special — no scrolling through hundreds of options required.

How It Began

One Dinner Party. A Hundred Questions.

In October 2022, Laura spent an entire evening putting together a Halloween tablescape for a neighborhood dinner party. By the end of the night, every single guest had their phone out asking where each piece came from — the candlesticks, the linen napkins, the smoked salt on the cheese board.

She spent the next hour texting product links. That frustration — beautiful objects with no easy way to discover and share them — became a newsletter. The newsletter grew into Savor & Simplify: a curated guide organized around seasons, occasions, and the moments that make a home feel truly lived in.

Every pick is something Laura has researched, tested, or styled herself. She earns a small commission when you shop through her links, at no extra cost to you. That's what keeps the lights on and the collections coming.

The Curation Process

Every Pick Earns Its Place.

For every product that makes it into a collection, Laura researches dozens that don't. She considers quality, styling versatility, value at each price tier, and whether it photographs the way it actually looks in person.

Collections are organized into three tiers — a Simplified Pick (best value), a Signature Pick (the one she'd reach for herself), and a Splurge Pick (when you really want to impress). You choose what fits your budget; every tier is worth it.

Disclosure: Savor & Simplify participates in affiliate programs including Amazon Associates, Caspari, and others. When you click a link and make a purchase, Laura earns a small commission at no additional cost to you. She only recommends things she genuinely loves.

What We Stand For

Our Values

Honest Curation

Every pick is something Laura has genuinely researched, tested, or styled. No sponsored placements. No brands paying to be featured. If it's here, she loves it.

Three Tiers, Every Time

Good, Better, Best. Every category has a budget pick, a signature pick, and a splurge pick — so you can build the look you want at whatever you can spend.

Seasonal Always

A new collection drops every three weeks. The calendar runs Halloween through Labor Day — over a dozen seasons, every year, in one place.

Radically Transparent

Affiliate links are disclosed on every page, every pick. Laura earns a small commission when you shop through her links. No extra cost to you — ever.

Community First

The newsletter is where Laura's most personal picks live — including items that don't make the public collections. It's free, and it ships twice a month.

Built to Last

Laura doesn't recommend things she'd replace next season. Every pick has to earn its place in someone's home for years — not just for the photo.
