Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days. Expedited (1–2 days) is available at checkout. Orders over $75 ship free via standard.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery for unused items in original packaging. Pantry goods are final sale once opened for food safety reasons.

Do you offer gift wrapping?

Yes — select gift wrapping at checkout for $6. We'll include a handwritten note on recycled card stock and wrap everything in our signature linen ribbon.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Orders can be modified or cancelled within 2 hours of placement. After that, we may have already packed your order — contact us quickly and we'll do our best.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently we ship within the continental US, Alaska, and Hawaii. International shipping is planned for 2026 — join our newsletter to be first to know.

How do I apply to be a producer partner?

We love discovering small makers. Fill out the contact form above and select "Producer Partnership" — include what you make and your location. We review every submission.
